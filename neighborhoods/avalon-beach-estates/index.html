Avalon Beach Estates

A mix of Florida beach houses and contemporary builds with private beach facilities, a pool, tennis, and a clubhouse.

Avalon Beach Estates sits between the gulf and the more commercial stretch of Highway 98. It is a proper neighborhood — streets, yards, a clubhouse — used by both full-time residents and vacation owners. Many homes look at the water or sit a short walk from the private beach. Architecture ranges from classic low-country porches to newer glass-and-stucco contemporaries.

Who it suits

Households who want amenities and neighbors, without a high-rise HOA.

Pricing context

Family homes often start around a million; gulf-front estates are several times that.

At a glance

  • Private beach, pool, tennis, clubhouse
  • Primary residences and vacation homes
  • Gulf views on select streets
  • Community rather than a tower
